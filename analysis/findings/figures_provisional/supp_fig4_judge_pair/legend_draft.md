**Supplementary Figure 4 | Two judges on the same answers, every metric.** Judge 1, devstral-small-2:24b; judge 2, qwen3.8:27b, at the same settings, both on the final key. RLM-5, RLM-10 and RLM-20 are our RLM. **a**, Scores of the two judges on the same answers, one grid per metric: gemini-3-flash's answers to the 86 answerable questions, six strategies, three draws; for refusal, its answers to the 39 out-of-scope questions. Columns, judge 1's score (0 to 5); rows, judge 2's score; cell number and colour (dark blue, low; yellow, high), share of the metric's answers (%). Faithfulness with passages, the judge's prompt also holds the retrieved passages. A failed answer scores 0 under both judges; an answer either judge failed to score is left out of that metric. **b**, Change in pass rate (correctness 4 or more) from single pass, in percentage points, for each generator (rows) and strategy (columns); single pass taken on the same questions and draws as the strategy. Filled dot, judge 1; open dot, judge 2, drawn over the filled dot where the two coincide; vertical line, no change. Empty, no second-judge rows: gemini-2.5-pro's multi-hop, RLM-5 and RLM-20. 'Same family', qwen3-coder:30b is of judge 2's model family. **c**, Agreement of the two judges per metric (rows): quadratic-weighted kappa on scores 0 to 5, Spearman's ρ, and Cohen's kappa on pass or fail at marks 4 and 3 (refusal, kappa at 4 only), each over 10,000 resamples of the questions. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; n = 86 questions (refusal, 39).
