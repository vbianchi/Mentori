# Supplementary Figure 16, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S481_fig6_d138.py` from the plotted numbers. Data stamp: PROVISIONAL: 2,420 of 2,420 attempts, 11 of 11 variants complete, records of 2026-11-04 08:35 CET; after the S4 reruns (D75, D80 V1, D81).

**Supplementary Figure 16 | Structured prompting, thinking setting and dataset, model by model.** An attempt is one model variant (a model at one thinking setting), one prompting strategy and one of 20 data-analysis operations, run once (165 attempts run again after a rule change, the later run counted). An attempt passes when its code returns values matching the reference; 27 attempts on one survival operation pass on running their stored code again (no model call). **a**, Per model variant, grey dots, one per operation: its pass share under the 4 structured strategies minus under the 3 free-form ones (percentage points). Box, quartiles; thick line, median; whiskers, range; n = 20 operations per box. Right, operations below / above 0. Rows: Gemini, then open-weight variants, each high to low by free-form pass rate. **b**, One bar per thinking setting of gpt-oss:20b, gemini-2.5-pro and gemini-3-flash, its 220 attempts split by outcome (% of attempts). Wrong values: values returned, not matching the reference. No result: no values returned. Tool or call failure, other: a malformed or failed call, no code, a harness error or a timeout after returning values. Gemini low: gemini-2.5-pro with a thinking budget of 128 tokens per call, gemini-3-flash at the minimal level. Right: medians per attempt of output tokens (gpt-oss:20b's may include thinking, which it does not count), thinking tokens (n.r., not recorded) and wall time (s; gpt-oss:20b local, Gemini through Google's API). **c**, Pass rate per model variant and dataset (left, airway RNA-seq; right, NCCTG lung survival; 10 operations each) under free-form (circle; 3 strategies, 30 attempts) and structured prompting (diamond; 4 strategies, 40 attempts), joined by a grey line where the diamond's centre lies outside the circle. Gap, structured minus free-form (percentage points). Rows: Gemini, then open-weight variants, each high to low by free-form pass rate.
