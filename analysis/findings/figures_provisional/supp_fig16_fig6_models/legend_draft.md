**Supplementary Figure 16 | Structured prompting, thinking setting and dataset, model by model.** Attempts as in Figure 7: one model variant, one prompting strategy and one of 20 operations, run once (165 attempts run again after a rule change, the later run counted; n = 2,420 attempts); 27 attempts on one survival operation are re-scored by running their stored code again (no model call). **a**, Per model variant, each operation's pass share under the 4 structured strategies minus under the 3 free-form ones (percentage points). Box, quartiles; thick line, median; whiskers, range; grey dots, one per operation (n = 20 per box). Right column, operations below / above 0. Rows: Gemini, then open-weight, each by free-form pass rate, ties by passes over all 11 strategies. **b**, One bar per thinking setting of the 3 models run at more than one (n = 220 attempts each), split by outcome as in Figure 7c, length stops shown apart (none here). Segments too narrow for one whole mark of their pattern are colour only. Gemini low: gemini-2.5-pro with a thinking budget of 128 tokens per call, gemini-3-flash at the minimal level. Right: medians per attempt. Thinking tokens where recorded (n.r., not recorded). gpt-oss:20b records no thinking count, so its output tokens may include thinking. Wall time: gpt-oss:20b local, Gemini through Google's API. **c**, Pass rate per variant and dataset under free-form (circle, n = 30 attempts: 3 strategies on 10 operations) and structured prompting (diamond, n = 40), joined by a grey line where the diamond's centre lies outside the circle. Right, structured minus free-form (percentage points). Rows: Gemini, then open-weight, each by free-form pass rate (a tie broken by the pass rate over all strategies).
