- **all panels**: Concept recall is D181's reading (D186 (4); findings/recompute/L3_d181). Judge rows are read through scripts/judge_rows_v25.py: a key v2.5 row where one exists, else key v2.4. Papers is MiniCheck precision against the key's source papers, draw 1, over the sentences MiniCheck scored that a Claimify-style selection keeps (D208; Metropolitansky and Larson 2025, arXiv 2502.10855, Selection stage): S564 asked devstral-small-2:24b, at the lock's instrument settings, whether each sentence holds a specific and verifiable proposition, and a sentence S564 judged leaves Papers only on "does not contain"; a partial verdict keeps the original sentence, which MiniCheck scored. scripts/minicheck_filter.py then leaves out a sentence matching its source-list pattern; no heading filter is applied (D245). S564 run 6934ed46ccab: 13,740 sentences of the answers read, 10,578 selected. S564 gave no verdict on 4 sentences (length), 8 sentences (unparseable) in 12 answers; these are kept as selected, as S564 records them. 67 answers with a stored value were not among the answers S564 judged (7 answerable, 60 false_premise) and have no Papers value. 1 answer has no sentence left and no Papers value. Of 573 rows with a stored value, 441 changed. MiniCheck checks only the key's source papers (S470_l1_signals.py, papers stage), so a claim taken correctly from another paper of the corpus counts as unsupported; how often that happens (review finding S12) is not stated here, because it comes from the review's work script findings/recompute/findings_review_2026-10-30/scripts/out/D03_work/a14.py, which no step of this build runs. The judge's prompt holds the expert answer and the expected concepts (experiments/scorers/judge_passages.py:8-11), and the judge was chosen partly for following concept recall, so part of the agreement between Judge and Concepts is built in (review finding O12).
- **a**: Judge: per question the share of three draws at 4 or more, one grey dot per question, stacked five high. Concepts, mean of three draws; Papers, draw 1. Quartiles by numpy percentile (linear); whiskers to the smallest and largest value (hs.box_stats, whis range).
- **b**: Each signal's rank is rankdata of minus its six means on the W questions (S473.compute; S06_metric_ablation.py:95-98), 1 best, ties sharing the average rank. A mix's rank is rankdata of the mean of its members' ranks. The Judge value here is mean correctness 0 to 5 over three draws, not a's pass rate.
- **c**: Per W question where both values exist: judge correctness (0 to 5, not a's pass mark) and concept recall averaged over three draws, Papers draw 1. A strictly higher value wins; equal within 1e-9 is a tie. A count too narrow for its segment prints just right of the bar if it is the last segment; any other is not printed and stays in the sheet: RLM-5 Papers tie 7, RLM-10 Papers tie 4, RLM-20 Papers tie 3.
- **d**: Faithfulness from the judge's call with passages; pass = correctness of the plain call at 4 or more. Each row pools the answers of all three draws (no mean is taken): its bar shows what share of those answers scored 0 to 5, and n at right counts them. Of the 1,548 answers of the 86 questions, 1,539 are drawn: 6 calls with passages ended in an error, every one a request longer than the judge's context, which the lock never cuts (LOCK.toml truncate = false), and 3 answers have no text. Calls longer than the judge's context are refused (6). Supplementary Figure 12d leaves out the same answers.
- **e**: All pairs where both values exist, draw 1. Column 1: judge correctness, plain call. Column 2: judge faithfulness, call with passages. RAGAS rows of status ok or ok_with_nulls, RAGChecker rows of status ok. Simple scores from L1's overlap stage. Box: quartiles of the 10,000 question resamples, not of the data. Open mark: within one question each signal is ranked, centred, and Pearson's r taken over all pairs. RAGAS and RAGChecker have no comparison with a person, because the rated answers keep no passages; Supplementary Figure 3 sets simple evaluators against a person.
