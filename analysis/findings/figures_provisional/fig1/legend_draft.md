# Figure 1, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S456_fig1_design.py figure` from the design files.

**Figure 1 | The three signals that can read an answer, and what the study varied.** **a**, Each answer is read by up to three signals, each against its own reference and reported on its own; under each, the tool that scores it and its readouts. Judge: correctness, pass at a score of 4 or more; refusal of an out-of-scope question, refused at 3 or more; faithfulness, scored without or with the retrieved passages. Concepts: share of the key's expected concepts found in the answer; a concept's content words that also occur in the question are not counted. Papers: MiniCheck's share of the answer's claims supported by the key's source papers. **b**, The settings varied, by the pipeline stage they act on: indexing (INDEX), retrieval (RETRIEVE), generation (GENERATE) and judging (JUDGE). Each number is a setting's number of levels. 51,579 answers, all draws, including the model-selection and retrieval runs; at least 1,182,144 model calls, excluding those runs and checks, smoke tests and dropped models. Supplementary Table 2 names the models.
