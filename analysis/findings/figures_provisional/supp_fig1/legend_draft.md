**Supplementary Figure 1 | How the experiments of the study connect.**

Each white box is one experiment of the study (n = 22 experiments); the shaded box is the evaluation corpus they share. A box gives the experiment, its design and its draws. The strategy replication's draws: 3 draws, RLM on a third; 1 + 2 draws for gemini-2.5-pro; 3 draws for claude-haiku-4-5. The key under the graph says what each kind of draw means. Sent the same seed, a local model nearly always repeats its answer, so its draws differ through their seeds; Gemini's draws are independent samples. The retrieval optimization and model selection were made before the experiments reported here and not run again; retrieval optimization used its own queries, and the model selection box counts the language models that made a call in this study, from the call logs. An arrow from one box to another means that the second experiment uses the output or a choice of the first. Zhang RLM is the RLM package released by the method's authors (rlms 0.1.3), drawn as Zhang RLM-10 in Figure 3 at its cap of 10 turns; RLM is Mentor::i's own implementation. RAG, retrieval-augmented generation.
