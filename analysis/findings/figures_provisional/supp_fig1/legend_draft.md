**Supplementary Figure 1 | How the experiments of the study connect.**

Each white box is one of the study's 22 experiments; the shaded box is the evaluation corpus they share. The five columns, left to right: corpus and choices; core experiments; built on the core; ablation; replication and reuse. A box gives the experiment, its design and its draws. The strategy replication's draws: 3 draws, RLM on a third; 1 + 2 draws for gemini-2.5-pro; 3 draws for claude-haiku-4-5. The component benchmarks' draws: 1 draw, 3 draws for gemini-3-flash. The component benchmark and distiller boxes count the items stored, 18 and 3 of them on questions later replaced in the answer key and left out of the analyses. The key under the graph says what each kind of draw means. Sent the same seed, a local model nearly always repeats its answer, so its draws differ through their seeds; Gemini's draws are independent samples. The retrieval optimization and model selection were made before the experiments reported here and not run again; retrieval optimization used its own queries, and the model selection box counts the language models that made a call in this study, from the call logs. An arrow from one box to another means that the second experiment uses the output or a choice of the first. Zhang RLM is the RLM package released by the method's authors (rlms 0.1.3), drawn as Zhang RLM-10 in Figure 3 at its cap of 10 turns; RLM is Mentor::i's own implementation. RAG, retrieval-augmented generation.
