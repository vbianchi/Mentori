**Supplementary Figure 6 | Replication on other generators in full.** Judged by devstral-small-2:24b on the final answer key; a pass is judge score 4 or more. **a**, For each generator, two rows: verified pass and RLM-10, each the generator's pass share with that strategy minus its pass share with single pass, per answerable question, in percentage points. Bar, the share of questions (%) where the strategy passed fewer draws than single pass (vermilion, left of 0) and more (blue, right of 0); ties are not drawn (counts in the Source Data). Diamond, the mean of the questions' differences in percentage points; line, its two-sided 95% percentile interval over 10,000 question resamples. A question counts in a row when the generator holds both strategies on it. n = 86 questions per row, and n = 29 for RLM-10 of gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b. A share is over the draws a generator holds: n = 3 draws, except gemini-2.5-pro (draw 1 only outside the fixed 45-question subset) and the local generators' RLM runs (the 45-question subset only). **b**, Rank of the six strategies by pass rate within each generator (1 is the highest, yellow; a tie shares the higher rank), on the answerable questions every strategy of that generator answered: n = 86 for gemini-3-flash, claude-haiku-4-5 and gemini-2.5-pro, n = 29 for gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b. **c**, RLM-10 minus single pass on one-paper (blue square) and many-paper (orange diamond) questions, by generation model, exploratory; Figure 3e draws their difference. Mark, the mean of the questions' differences; bar, its two-sided 95% percentile interval over 10,000 question resamples. Rows as Figure 3e: top, the reference; 2 to 4, the prespecified test; below the gap, models outside it; main judge unless the row names the second judge (qwen3.8:27b). gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b, n = 3 draws (RLM-10: 1 draw on 17 of the many-paper questions), on n = 20 one-paper and n = 26 many-paper questions; the other rows on n = 60 and n = 26. n = 4 generators beside gemini-3-flash.
