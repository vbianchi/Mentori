# Supplementary Figure 6, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S523_supp_fig7_replication.py`.

**Supplementary Figure 6 | Replication on other generators in full.** Judged by devstral-small-2:24b on the final answer key; a pass is judge score 4 or more. **a**, For each generator, two rows: verified pass and RLM-10, each the generator's pass share with that strategy minus its pass share with single pass, per answerable question, over its draws (three, except gemini-2.5-pro, one draw outside the fixed 45-question subset (29 of them answerable), and the RLM runs of gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b, that subset only). Bars, the share of questions (%) where the strategy passed fewer draws than single pass (vermilion, left of 0) and more (blue, right of 0); ties are not drawn. Diamond, the mean of the questions' differences; line, its two-sided 95% percentile interval over 10,000 question resamples; dashed line, 0. A question counts in a row when the generator holds both strategies on it: n = 86 questions per row, except n = 29 questions for RLM-10 of gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b. **b**, Rank of six strategies (columns) by pass rate within each generator (rows), on the answerable questions every strategy of that generator answered; 1 is the highest and yellow, and a tie shares the higher rank. **c**, RLM-10 minus single pass, on one-paper (blue square) and many-paper (orange diamond) questions, per generator, exploratory. Mark, the mean of the questions' differences; bar, its two-sided 95% percentile interval over 10,000 question resamples; dashed line, 0. Top row, gemini-3-flash, the reference; rows 2 to 4, the prespecified test; below the gap, generators outside it. Main judge except the row naming the second judge (qwen3.8:27b). gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b use three draws (RLM-10: one draw on 17 of the many-paper questions), n = 20 one-paper and n = 26 many-paper questions; the other rows, n = 60 and n = 26 questions.
