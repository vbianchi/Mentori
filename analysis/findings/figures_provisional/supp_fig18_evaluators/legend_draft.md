# Supplementary Figure 18, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S542_supp_evaluators.py figure`. Data stamp: PROVISIONAL: 506 of 516 draw-1 answerable answers have the judge's score and all 16 tool scores; RAGAS 576 and RAGChecker 576 rows (K1); ranks on 75 questions.

**Supplementary Figure 18 | RAGAS, RAGChecker and BERTScore against the judge and the three signals.** Answers of the six strategies to the answerable questions, draw 1 (n = 516 answers, 86 questions; 506 hold the judge's score and every RAGAS and RAGChecker score, the other 10 lack one or more of them). RAGAS and RAGChecker ran on devstral-small-2:24b at the judge's settings. **a**, Spearman's ρ of the 13 scores not in Figure 6e with the judge's correctness and faithfulness, each over the answers holding both values (the answers per ρ are in sheet a's answers column): box, quartiles of 10,000 question resamples; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; open mark, ρ among the answers to one question. A negative ρ means agreement for hallucination, self-knowledge and both noise sensitivities. BERTScore is the F1 of roberta-large over windows of at most 510 word pieces, each starting 255 pieces after the last, so no answer or key is cut (n = 514 answers with correctness, 511 with faithfulness). **b**, Strategy rank by each score (1, best; ties share the mean rank) on n = 75 questions; right, Spearman's ρ of each row's ranks with the judge's at 4 or more.
