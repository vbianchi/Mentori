# Supplementary Figure 18, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S542_supp_evaluators.py figure`.

**Supplementary Figure 18 | RAGAS, RAGChecker and BERTScore against the judge and the three signals.** Answers of the six strategies to the 86 answerable questions, draw 1; 10 of the 516 answers lack the judge's score or one or more RAGAS or RAGChecker scores. RAGAS and RAGChecker ran on devstral-small-2:24b at the judge's settings. BERTScore is the F1 of roberta-large over windows of at most 510 word pieces, each starting 255 pieces after the last, so no answer or key is cut. **a**, Spearman's ρ (horizontal axis) of 13 scores (rows: 3 RAGAS, 9 RAGChecker and windowed BERTScore) with the judge's correctness (left) and faithfulness (right), each over the answers holding both values. Box, quartiles of 10,000 resamples of the questions; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; open circle, ρ among the answers to one question; vertical line, ρ = 0. A negative ρ means agreement for hallucination, self-knowledge and both noise sensitivities. n = 86 questions per ρ. **b**, Rank of the six strategies (columns) by the mean of each score (rows: the judge's pass share at 4 or more and at 3 or more, Concepts, Papers, 5 RAGAS and 11 RAGChecker scores) on the 75 questions where every strategy holds all 20 scores. 1 is the best and yellow, 6 the worst and dark blue; the best is the lowest for hallucination, self-knowledge and both noise sensitivities; ties share the mean rank. Right column, Spearman's ρ of each row's ranks with the ranks by the judge at 4 or more.
