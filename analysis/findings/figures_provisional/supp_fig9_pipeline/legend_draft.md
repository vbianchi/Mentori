**Supplementary Figure 9 | Mentor::i's question-answering pipeline.**

The analyser decides, in one model call, whether a question is answered directly, in one further call without tools, or planned. The planner writes a goal and ordered steps, one tool each; at most 10 run. Document steps go to the smart query router, which picks a retrieval strategy by keyword rules and index size, without a model call. The distiller condenses a step output longer than 209,712 characters. The supervisor scores each step from 0 to 100; below 70, the step's arguments are rewritten and the step reruns, up to 3 times. The synthesizer writes the answer from the question, the goal and each step's last output. Blue box, a model call; black box, no model call; grey dashed frame, what runs for each plan step; dashed vermilion box, an ablation set-up, drawn under or beside the step it changes. The 7 ablation set-ups are no analyser, the routing fix (factual questions planned), no planner, no supervisor, no synthesizer and every step distilled with Mentor::i's prompt or with the worked-example prompt, which adds two worked examples that keep every number.
