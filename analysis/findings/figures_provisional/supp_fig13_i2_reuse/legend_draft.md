# Supplementary Figure 13, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S570_supp_i2_reuse.py figure`.

**Supplementary Figure 13 | The pipeline and its evaluation on five plant-science papers.** The key covers five open-access papers on lettuce resistance to downy mildew. Questions 1 and 2 are extractive, 3 is a synthesis, 4 has a false premise and 5 is out of scope. Six strategies (single pass, multi-hop, verified pass and our RLM stopped at 5, 10 and 20 turns: RLM-5, RLM-10 and RLM-20) answered each question with gemini-3-flash in 3 draws, 90 answers in all. The judge (devstral-small-2:24b, plain call) scored every answer. Question 5 has no concept target. In three question-draws the RLMs returned the same text: questions 1 and 4 (all three RLMs, one draw each) and question 3 (RLM-5 and RLM-20, one draw). **a**, One tile per strategy (rows) and question (columns): the draws passed of 3, printed in the tile and coloured from 0 (dark blue) to 3 (yellow). Left block, judge correctness 4 or more; right block, 3 or more. For question 5 a tile counts the draws refused instead (refusal score 3 or more, the same in both blocks). **b**, Concept recall (loose matcher with the question rule) of the 72 answers to the first four questions, one row per strategy: one dot per question, the mean over its n = 3 draws; colour and shape, question; black line, the strategy's median of its n = 4 question means. **c**, Papers precision (MiniCheck against each question's source papers) of the 72 answers to the first four questions, one row per strategy: one dot per question, the mean over its draws with a value (n = 70 answers have one; the other 2 are longer than the encoder's 2,048-token input); colour and shape, question; black line, the strategy's median of its n = 4 question means.
