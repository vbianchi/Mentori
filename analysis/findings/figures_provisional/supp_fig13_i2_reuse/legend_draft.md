**Supplementary Figure 13 | The pipeline and its evaluation on five plant-science papers.** The key covers five open-access papers on lettuce resistance to downy mildew. Questions 1 and 2 are extractive, 3 is a synthesis, 4 has a false premise and 5 is out of scope. The six strategies of Figure 3 answered them with gemini-3-flash in n = 3 draws, n = 90 answers. They were scored as on the main benchmark. The judge (devstral-small-2:24b, plain call) scored every answer. Question 5 has no concept target, so Concepts and Papers scored the answers to questions 1 to 4 only. RLM-5, RLM-10 and RLM-20 are our RLM, stopped at those turns. **a**, Draws passed of 3 per question and strategy. Left, judge correctness 4 or more; right, 3 or more. For question 5 a tile counts draws refused (refusal score 3 or more, the same in both blocks). Answers stored, judged and with a Concepts and a Papers value, per strategy, are in the Source Data (sheet a, coverage rows). **b**, Concept recall (loose matcher with the question rule, as in Figure 6a) of the four answerable and false-premise questions: one dot per question and strategy, the mean over its 3 draws (n = 72 answers). Colour and shape, question. **c**, Papers precision (MiniCheck against each question's source papers) of the same answers, as in **b**, the mean over the draws with a value (n = 70 answers; the other 2 are longer than the encoder's 2,048-token input and have no value). In three question-draws the RLMs returned the same text: question 1 and question 4 (all three, one draw each) and question 3 (RLM-5 and RLM-20, one draw). In **b** and **c**, per strategy, black line: median of the 4 question means.
