**Supplementary Figure 8 | Generation strategies against single pass across corpus sizes.** One cell per strategy (rows: multi-hop, verified pass and RLM stopped at 5, 10 and 20 turns: RLM-5, RLM-10 and RLM-20) and per index (columns). Each index holds 5, 10, 20 or 50 core papers (column groups) and 0, 1 or 3 distractor papers for every core paper (columns). Answers are from gemini-3-flash, one draw, to the answerable questions whose source papers are all in that index, with the strategy's and single pass's answers both judged. Each cell gives the strategy's pass rate minus single pass's on the same questions, in percentage points, printed in the cell and coloured from −20 (dark blue) through 0 (grey) to +20 (yellow). Upper block: pass is judge correctness 4 or more; lower block (relaxed): 3 or more. Frame inside a cell: the two-sided 95% percentile interval of the difference, from 10,000 resamples of the questions, excludes 0; n = 22 questions per index at 5 core papers, 44 at 10, 86 at 20 and 86 at 50.
