# Supplementary Figure 17, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S457_fig2_panels.py figure` from the plotted numbers.

**Supplementary Figure 17 | Outcomes, tokens, time and turns per answer for each generation strategy.** Each set-up answered the 135 questions three times with gemini-3-flash on the 20-paper index (405 answers). Matched budget is a single pass with passages matching RLM-10's input tokens; RLM is Mentor::i's own and Zhang RLM the method authors' package (rlms 0.1.3), each with its turn cap (5, 10 and 20) after the dash. **a**, Answers that did not end in a plain answer, stacked by kind. Right: answers that did; answers with no text; and, of the 117 answers per set-up to the 39 out-of-scope questions, those judged refused (refusal score 3 or more), among those cut at the turn limit (RLM-5) or by the closing turn (multi-hop), and the rest (no judge row: not refused). **b**, Tokens per answer (prompt, output and thinking, verifier's included), log scale. Grey dots, question means over three draws; box, interquartile range; line, median; whiskers, range; n = 135 questions per set-up. **c**, Clock time per answer (s) less the local verifier wait, log scale. Grey dots, question means over three draws; box, interquartile range; line, median; whiskers, range; n = 135 questions per set-up. **d**, Model-call time per answer (s), summed over calls, log scale; blank for Zhang RLM (parallel sub-calls). Grey dots, question means over three draws; box, interquartile range; line, median; whiskers, range; n = 135 questions per set-up. **e**, Turns used by the six RLM set-ups: cell colour, answers of the set-up's 405 using that many turns (log scale); no cell, none; black outline, turn cap. **f**, Verified pass, time per answer (s), linear scale: clock time with the verifier wait, and the wait alone. Grey dots, question means over three draws; box, interquartile range; line, median; whiskers, range; n = 135 questions.
