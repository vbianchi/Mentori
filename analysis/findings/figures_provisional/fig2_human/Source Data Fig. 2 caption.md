Source Data Fig. 2. The numbers behind Figure 2, one sheet per panel. The source data for Figure 2 is in Supplementary Data 3. Data stamp: two raters, 49 of 49 answers drawn.

- Fig. 2a: 9 rows. One row per pair of bands, the judge's correctness score (judge_band: 0-2, 3, 4-5) and the raters' correctness score (rater_band: <3, 3 to <4, ≥4); answers, the number of answers in that pair.
- Fig. 2b: 9 rows. One row per band of the raters' faithfulness score (rater_band) and band of the judge's faithfulness score (judge_band); answers, the number in that pair; share_pct, their share of the rater band (%); band_n, the answers in the rater band.
- Fig. 2c: 49 rows. One row per rated answer (eval_id): each rater's correctness and faithfulness (r1_, r2_), the raters' mean (raters_), the raters' faithfulness band (band_faithfulness), the judge's correctness and faithfulness scores (judge_) and MiniCheck against the key (minicheck_key_precision, 0 to 1).
- Fig. 2d: 2001 rows. First row 'all answers': Spearman's rho of the raters' faithfulness with the judge (rho_judge) and with MiniCheck (rho_minicheck), their difference D = rho_judge minus rho_minicheck with its two-sided 95% percentile interval (D_ci95_low, D_ci95_high), each also as sign and size (d_, ci_low_, ci_high_), and the most resamples in one hexagon (hex_max). Then one row per answer resample (resample 1 to 2,000) with rho_judge, rho_minicheck and D.
- Fig. 2e: 26 rows. One row per dimension (correctness, faithfulness), statistic and pair (judge with raters, raters with each other, Delta = the first minus the second), and one 'bias' row per dimension (the judge's score minus the raters'): point, the estimate on all answers; ci95_low and ci95_high, the two-sided 95% percentile interval; q25, median, q75, the box; finite_resamples, resamples with a value.
- Fig. 2f: 24 rows. One row per pass mark (4, 3), signal or rule (row: Judge, Concepts, Papers, at least 1, 2 and 3 of 3) and statistic (accuracy, kappa) against the raters' correctness: n, answers; point, ci95_low, q25, median, q75, ci95_high and finite_resamples as in Fig. 2e. On the accuracy rows of Judge, Concepts and Papers, a two-sided exact McNemar test of the 2 of 3 rule against that signal: rule_right_single_wrong and rule_wrong_single_right, the discordant answers; p_two_sided; favours_rule, true when the rule is right on more of them and p_two_sided is below 0.05.
