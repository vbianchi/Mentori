**Figure 2 | Agreement of three automated signals with expert raters.**

Two authors rated 49 answers; the raters' score is their mean. **a**, Correctness: answers per pair of bands of the judge's and the raters' scores; outlined cells, agreeing bands. **b**, Faithfulness: share of each judge band within each band of the raters' score; n above each bar. **c**, MiniCheck against the key by the raters' faithfulness band; dots, every answer. **d**, Spearman's ρ of the raters' faithfulness with the judge's against that with MiniCheck, over 2,000 resamples of the answers; colour, resamples per hexagon. **e**, Agreement, judge with raters and raters with each other: quadratic-weighted kappa, Spearman's ρ and Cohen's kappa at pass marks 4 and 3. Delta, the first minus the second. **f**, Accuracy and Cohen's kappa of Judge, Concepts (concept recall) and Papers, passing at 0.5, and the rules at least 1, 2 and 3 of 3, against the raters' correctness at the same pass mark. Boxes, quartiles; lines, medians; whiskers, range in c and two-sided 95% percentile interval of the resamples in e and f. Diamonds, the estimate on all answers. Dashed lines, reference only. Source Data gives d's difference and f's McNemar tests, both two-sided. Verified pass holds 5 of the answers. n = 49 answers; 48 in f.
