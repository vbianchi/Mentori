**Figure 2 | Agreement of three automated signals with expert raters.**

Two authors rated 49 answers; the raters' score is their mean. Verified pass holds 5 of the answers. **a**, Correctness: answers per pair of bands of the judge's score (rows) and the raters' score (columns), printed in each cell; outlined cells, agreeing bands. **b**, Faithfulness: share of each judge band within each band of the raters' score; the number of answers in each band is printed above its bar. **c**, MiniCheck against the key by the raters' faithfulness band; dots, every answer; box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, range; n = 49 rated answers. **d**, Spearman's ρ of the raters' faithfulness with the judge's against that with MiniCheck, over 2,000 resamples of the answers; colour, resamples per hexagon; diamond, the estimate on all answers; diagonal line, equal ρ. Source Data gives the difference of the two ρ with its two-sided 95% percentile interval. n = 49 rated answers. **e**, Agreement, judge with raters and raters with each other: quadratic-weighted kappa, Spearman's ρ and Cohen's kappa at pass marks 4 and 3, over 2,000 resamples of the answers. Delta, the first minus the second. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; diamond, the estimate on all answers; dashed line, kappa 0.6, reference only. n = 49 rated answers. **f**, Accuracy and Cohen's kappa of Judge; Concepts (concept recall) and Papers passing at 0.5; and the rules at least 1, 2 and 3 of 3, against the raters' correctness at the same pass mark, over 2,000 resamples of the answers. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; dashed line, accuracy 0.5, reference only. Source Data gives two-sided McNemar tests. n = 48 rated answers that have a Papers score.
