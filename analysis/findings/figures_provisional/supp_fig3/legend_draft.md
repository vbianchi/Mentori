**Supplementary Figure 3 | Human evaluation in full.**

Two authors rated the 49 answers of Figure 2; the raters' score is their mean. **a**, Agreement of the two raters on faithfulness, six statistics (rows), over 2,000 answer resamples. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; dashed line, the gate on Krippendorff's alpha, 0.667. n = 49 rated answers. **b**, Spearman's ρ of seven signals (rows) with each rater's correctness (left) and faithfulness (right), over 2,000 answer resamples; grey, rater 1; white, rater 2; the second judge's row is exploratory, added after rating began. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval. n = 49 rated answers (48 in the Papers rows). **c**, Completeness: answers per pair of bands of the judge's score (rows) and the raters' score (columns); colour, count, yellow for more; outlined cells, agreeing bands. **d**, Accuracy (%) of the 2 of 3 rule (pass when at least two of judge, Concepts and Papers pass) against the raters' correctness, on answers with a Papers score, by Concepts cut-off (rows) and Papers cut-off (columns); blocks, the judge's and the raters' pass marks, 4 or 3; colour, accuracy, yellow for higher; outlined cell, the primary cell. **e**, Spearman's ρ of ROUGE-L, METEOR, number overlap, bge-m3 similarity and, for reference, the judge with the raters' correctness (blue) and faithfulness (orange), over 2,000 answer resamples; exploratory, added after rating began. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; diamond, the estimate on all answers. n = 49 rated answers (48 in the bge-m3 similarity row). **f**, Mean of the judge's score minus the raters' score (score points) for correctness and faithfulness, over 2,000 answer resamples; exploratory. Box, quartiles; line, median; whiskers, two-sided 95% percentile interval; solid line, no difference. n = 49 rated answers.
