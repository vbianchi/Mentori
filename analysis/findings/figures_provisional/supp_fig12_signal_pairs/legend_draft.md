# Supplementary Figure 12, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S543_supp_signal_pairs.py figure`. Data stamp: PROVISIONAL: 516 of 516 draw-1 answerable answers have a final row from all four scorers; numbers from S497's panel_data on the claim-filtered rows.

**Supplementary Figure 12 | Answers stating every concept; each strategy against single pass; Papers by the judge's verdict and by faithfulness.** Answers of gemini-3-flash to the answerable questions under six strategies (rows). Judge, correctness score against the answer key, from 0 to 5, with a pass at 4 or more; Concepts, concept recall against the key; Papers, MiniCheck precision against the key's source papers. **a**, Draw-1 answers holding all three scores that state every key concept, split by the judge's verdict: grey, passes; blue, fails. Numbers in and beside the bars, answers; right, all such answers of the row. **b**, Each strategy against single pass, question by question within the same draw: questions where the strategy scores higher minus questions where single pass scores higher, as a percentage of the questions where both hold a score. Blue circles, Judge correctness score, one per draw (three draws); orange squares, Concepts, one per draw; black diamonds, Papers, draw 1. **c**, Draw-1 answers holding all three scores, split by the judge's verdict and by Papers, high at or above the median of these answers pooled (0.783): blue, judge fails and Papers high; light grey, judge passes and Papers high; dark grey, judge fails and Papers low; pink, judge passes and Papers low. Numbers in the bars, answers; right, the row's answers. **d**, Papers of the draw-1 answers with a faithfulness score, split by faithfulness 5 and 0 to 4 (rows). Faithfulness score, from the judge reading the retrieved passages; an answer with no text, or whose call is longer than the judge's context, has none. Grey dots, answers; box, quartiles; black line, median; whiskers, range; n = 78, 7, 67, 18, 81, 5, 48, 35, 48, 33, 47 and 35 answers per box, top to bottom, printed at right.
