**Figure 3 | Answer strategies against single pass, by the judge's pass rate.** Eight strategies answered 135 questions with gemini-3-flash in 3 draws, judged by devstral-small-2:24b for judge correctness (pass, score 4 or more; relaxed, 3 or more). **a**, The 86 answerable questions (60 one-paper, 26 many-paper), by strategy; tile colour, draws passed at score 4. Right, time per answer less the local server's wait: grey dots, questions (mean of 3 draws); black, strategy mean. **b**, The 39 out-of-scope questions, by strategy; tile colour, draws refused (refusal score 3 or more). Right, refusal rate with two-sided 95% interval, question bootstrap, n = 39 out-of-scope questions; dashed, one-sided 95% bound at 0 or 100%; open diamond, a person's reading of draw 1. **c**, Answerable questions single pass passes at score 4 and the strategy fails (harmed, vermilion) or the reverse (helped, blue); dots, the 9 pairings of draws; bars, their mean. **d**, Share of draws passing against mean model calls, per strategy; grey dots, questions; black, all answerable; blue, one-paper; orange, many-paper; open, score 3; vermilion cross, matched budget. **e**, RLM-10's gain over single pass on many-paper minus one-paper questions, per generator or judge; lines, two-sided 95% intervals, question bootstrap. Rows 2 to 4, the prespecified test. Second judge, qwen3.8:27b. n = 60 one-paper and 26 many-paper answerable questions (n = 20 one-paper for gemma3:27b and qwen3-coder:30b). **f**, Strategy minus single pass (percentage points) by core papers in the index, draw 1: pass rate on answerable questions in scope and on the same 22, refusal rate on the 39 out-of-scope; thick frame, two-sided 95% interval excludes 0; colour capped at 20 points. n, the questions printed under each column; RLM-10 split rows, n = 15, 30, 60 and 60 one-paper and 7, 14, 26 and 26 many-paper questions.
