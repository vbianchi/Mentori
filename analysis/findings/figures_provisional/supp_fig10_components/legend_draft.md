Five open-weight models answered each item once and gemini-3-flash answered it on three draws. **a**, Supervisor score against the judge score of the same answer, one panel per model; the judge scores an answer on the final key, or gives the refusal score for out-of-scope questions. Boxes: median and quartiles; whiskers: the range; grey dots: one answer each; a judge score with fewer than 5 answers shows its dots and a tick at their median. ρ, Spearman's ρ of the two scores. llama3:8b sits right of the grey line because its context is 8,192 tokens. For gemini-3-flash, each answer's supervisor score is the mean of its three draws. devstral-small-2:24b is both one of the supervisor models and the judge. n = 194 answers per model; 190 for gpt-oss:20b and 191 for llama3:8b, whose missing answers are counted below. Answers that gave no supervisor score are left out and counted: gpt-oss:20b, 4. Failed calls are left out and counted: llama3:8b, 3 supervisor items (3 with a prompt longer than its context). **b**, Component score (%), one row per model and one column per component: analyser, decisions matching the label; planner, tools matching the label; distiller, numbers kept. The analyser's label counts sending an out-of-scope question to a direct answer as correct.
