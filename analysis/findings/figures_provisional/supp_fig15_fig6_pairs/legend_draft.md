# Supplementary Figure 15, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S481_fig6_d138.py` from the plotted numbers. Data stamp: PROVISIONAL: 2,420 of 2,420 attempts, 11 of 11 variants complete, records of 2026-11-04 08:35 CET; after the S4 reruns (D75, D80 V1, D81).

**Supplementary Figure 15 | Paired outcomes and net change of repeated against single attempts.** An attempt is one model variant (a model at one thinking setting), one prompting strategy and one of 20 data-analysis operations on two datasets (airway RNA-seq and NCCTG lung survival), run once (165 attempts run again after a rule change, the later run counted); 27 attempts on one survival operation are re-scored by running their stored code again. A pair is one variant on one operation under a repeated-attempt strategy and under the single-attempt strategy of its family. In grids a to e, rows are variants (Gemini, then open-weight, each by pooled free-form pass rate, high to low; filled dot, Gemini; open dot, open-weight; low, the lowest thinking setting) and columns are operations, numbered within each dataset (10 airway RNA-seq and 10 NCCTG lung survival). **a**, Free-form best of 3 against free-form: each tile is one pair, coloured by outcome (key above); header, pairs rescued and lost. **b**, Free-form combined against free-form: each tile is one pair, coloured by outcome (key above); header, pairs rescued and lost. **c**, Structured best of 3 against structured: each tile is one pair, coloured by outcome (key above); header, pairs rescued and lost. **d**, Structured combined against structured: each tile is one pair, coloured by outcome (key above); header, pairs rescued and lost. **e**, Structured x3 against structured: each tile is one pair, coloured by outcome (key above); header, pairs rescued and lost. **f**, Change in pass rate (points) of each repeated-attempt strategy (column) over the single-attempt strategy of its family, same variant and operation. Dot, one variant over 20 operations (filled, Gemini; open, open-weight); diamond, net change over all pairs; line, two-sided 95% percentile interval from 10,000 resamples of the 20 operations; n = 220 pairs per strategy.
