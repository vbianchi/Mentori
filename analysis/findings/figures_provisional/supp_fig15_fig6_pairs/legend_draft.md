# Supplementary Figure 15, title and legend (draft)

Written by `scripts/S481_fig6_d138.py` from the plotted numbers. Data stamp: PROVISIONAL: 2,420 of 2,420 attempts, 11 of 11 variants complete, records of 2026-11-04 08:35 CET; after the S4 reruns (D75, D80 V1, D81).

**Supplementary Figure 15 | Paired outcomes and net change of repeated against single attempts.** Attempts as in Figure 7: one model variant, one prompting strategy and one of 20 operations, run once (165 attempts run again after a rule change, the later run counted; n = 2,420 attempts); 27 attempts on one survival operation are re-scored by running their stored code again (no model call). Every pair of the five repeated-attempt contrasts (a to e) and their net change (f). In a to e, a tile is one model variant (row) on one operation (column; 10 airway RNA-seq and 10 NCCTG lung survival operations, numbered within each dataset), coloured by which of the two strategies passed. n = 220 pairs per contrast (11 variants × 20 operations). Rows: Gemini, then open-weight, each by pooled free-form pass rate, high to low. A dot before a variant's name marks a Gemini model (filled) or an open-weight model (open). Gemini low: its lowest thinking setting. **a**, Free-form best of 3 against free-form. **b**, Free-form combined against free-form. **c**, Structured best of 3 against structured. **d**, Structured combined against structured. **e**, Structured x3 against structured. **f**, Change in pass rate of each repeated-attempt strategy against the single-attempt strategy of its family, same variant and operation, one column per contrast of a to e. Dot, one variant over 20 operations; diamond, net change over n = 220 pairs; line, two-sided 95% percentile interval from 10,000 resamples of the 20 operations.
