## What it measures

For each of Figure 3's six strategies, on the 86 answerable questions and draws 1 to 3, the script counts the words of each answer (whitespace tokens of the answer text) in the generation records Figure 3 reads (S463's reader, with the 15 reruns of 18 October in place of the first records). An answer that failed (a generation error or no text) counts 0 words and is listed apart in results.json. Each question's words are the mean over its three draws. Each question's concept recall is copied from Source Data Fig. 3, sheet Fig. 3a, rows 'concept recall (not drawn): question (grey dot)'.

Per question it takes RLM-10 minus single pass in words and in concept recall, and gives Pearson r between the two differences with a two-sided 95% percentile interval over 10,000 resamples of the questions (seed 20261013). RLM-5 and RLM-20 against single pass are the secondary rows. It runs no test.
