# Does the heading rule change any conclusion about Papers?

## In plain English

No rule of the 203 reverses the order of the strategies. Where no heading filter puts one strategy clearly above another, every rule keeps it above or makes the two tied.
 What moves is ties. 58 rules change whether two strategies count as tied: single_pass and verified_pass on 58 rules; multi_hop and rlm_5 on 1 rule; multi_hop and rlm_20 on 1 rule; rlm_5 and rlm_10 on 1 rule.



With no heading filter, RLM answers' Papers is -0.028 against the other three strategies' (interval -0.072 to +0.013), so RLM is tied with them. Across all rules the gap runs from -0.053 to -0.012: every rule lowers RLM more than the others, because RLM answers carry more headings. 
On 78 of the 203 rules the interval no longer reaches zero and RLM becomes clearly below the others; they are listed under "Where the RLM gap moves". On the rest, the best rules of S560 among them, it stays a tie, some only just.
